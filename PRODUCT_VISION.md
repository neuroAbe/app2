# CatchFeelings - Product Vision

## 🎮 Concept: Sims x Pokemon Dating Game

CatchFeelings combines the best elements of **The Sims** (life simulation, relationships) and **Pokemon** (exploration, encounters, collection) into a unique dating experience.

---

## 🎯 Core Vision

**"Explore. Encounter. Connect."**

Instead of endless swiping, users explore a vibrant 2D world, meet real people through random encounters, and build meaningful relationships through shared activities and quests.

---

## 🕹️ Pokemon-Inspired Elements

### 1. Exploration & Discovery
- **Top-down 2D world** - Navigate towns, parks, cafes, and special locations
- **Different zones** - Each area has different types of people and activities
- **Random encounters** - Meet people as you explore (proximity-based)
- **Map & navigation** - Fast travel between discovered locations

### 2. Collection & Progress
- **"Catch" connections** - Match with people you meet in the wild
- **Connection collection** - Build your network of friends and matches
- **Quest system** - Daily and weekly quests (visit 3 cafes, meet 5 new people)
- **Badges & achievements** - Unlock titles and rewards
- **Levels & XP** - Progress through the game by being social

### 3. Battle System (Reimagined)
- **Conversation challenges** - Ice breakers and conversation starters
- **Compatibility tests** - Fun quizzes to find common ground
- **Mini-games** - Play together to build rapport (trivia, word games)

## 🎮 Gameplay Loop

### Daily Flow
1. **Wake up** → Check messages, review match suggestions
2. **Explore** → Walk around town, visit locations
3. **Encounter** → Meet random users in the wild
4. **Interact** → Start conversations, play mini-games
5. **Match** → If both interested, add to connections
6. **Plan dates** → Schedule activities together
7. **Build relationships** → Track progress, unlock deeper conversations
8. **Complete quests** → Earn XP, badges, and rewards

### Progression
- **Level 1-10**: Beginner - Learn the basics, meet people casually
- **Level 11-25**: Explorer - Unlock new locations and activities
- **Level 26-50**: Social Butterfly - Access exclusive events
- **Level 51+**: Legend - Mentor new users, host events

## 🛡️ Safety-First Design

### Built-in Safety
- ✅ **Age verification** (18+ only)
- ✅ **Email & phone verification**
- ✅ **Photo verification** (optional)
- ✅ **Report & block system**
- ✅ **AI moderation** (toxic message detection)
- ✅ **Safe meeting suggestions** (public places only)

### Privacy Controls
- Choose who can see you (friends only, nearby only, everyone)
- Hide your real-time location
- Anonymous mode for exploring
- Block list automatically filters encounters
